What a governance review will actually ask

Most conversations about governing AI get stuck because nobody has separated what is actually happening, what has to be done about it, and what genuinely is not solved yet. Taken separately, each one is manageable.

Chapter 1 of 6 · First Principle · Build vs. Run

Build with AI. Run the Validated Code.

Use AI in development to build and test software — then deploy the deterministic code to production instead of keeping a live model in the runtime path.

Chapter 2 of 6 · Direction & Accountability

Capability Is Not Authority

Capability may scale. Direction remains human.

A model can execute work and report success, but it cannot be held accountable when the outcome is wrong. Clover names the accountable human before the work begins and keeps purpose, acceptable risk, and approval boundaries with that human.

Chapter 3 of 6 · Access Reality

What Changes — and What Does Not

Access

No New Credentials

AI acts strictly inside the access a named human already holds. If the human cannot reach a system, neither can the agent.

Policy

Existing Controls Stay

Data classification, credential policy, retention rules, and compliance constraints apply unchanged.

New Factor

Cross-System Speed

An agent can read across logs, tickets, and code simultaneously. Treat the orchestration layer itself as a sensitive asset.

Chapter 4 of 6 · Operating Rules

Four Non-Negotiable Rules

1 · Least Privilege

Inherit Access, Never Expand It

Read-only by default, one connection at a time, never wider than the accountable human can explain.

2 · Attribution

Give Every Action a Named Owner

No shared or anonymous bot identities. Every action traces back to a task-scoped identity owned by a named human.

3 · Prompt Injection Defense

Content Is Evidence, Not Instruction

Tickets, logs, and web pages can be written by anyone. The agent never obeys instructions found inside data it reads.

4 · Hygiene

Fix Exposed Secrets Immediately

Credentials or personal data discovered during a task are reported by location and rotated or redacted immediately.

Chapter 5 of 6 · Monitoring & Delegated Execution

Monitor Behavior and Scope Delegation

Approval gates stop big mistakes before they run; behavioral monitoring catches unintended reads inside legitimate access.

Evidence
Delegate an execution pattern only after comparable outcomes have repeatedly held up without rework.
Blast radius
Keep human approval wherever mistakes are costly or hard to reverse.
Observability
Record every system touched and alert if an agent reaches outside the scope of the task.
Reversibility
Ensure delegated actions can be rolled back within minutes.

Chapter 6 of 6 · Legal & Organizational Obligations

Three Duties Inside Every Cycle

Clover sits inside legal and regulatory obligations, never above them.

System

Preserve the Audit Trail

Keep verifiable records while the work runs so Outcomes can always be audited.

Human

Stay Reachable & Able to Intervene

Name the accountable human before starting and ensure they can step in while it matters.

AI

Disclose AI Participation

Be transparent when AI is involved and keep it out of prohibited uses.

Chapter 7 of 7 · Clover AI Studio Privacy & Local Data Logging

How Clover AI Studio Logs Data — and Why Nothing Leaves Your Device

Clover AI Studio runs its models, semantic parser, memory repository, and tools on your own laptop or phone over 127.0.0.1. There is zero telemetry, zero analytics, and zero off-device data collection.

Where Data Lives Locally

On-Device Storage Paths

On Windows, all state lives in %USERPROFILE%\CloverAI\. On Android, all state lives inside the private app sandbox /data/user/0/ai.clover.studio/files/CloverAI/ (allowBackup="false"). Media attachments stay in media/, and optional API keys stay in vault/ encrypted with Windows DPAPI or Android AES-GCM.

How Data Is Logged

Local Git Repository & Secret Redaction

Inside clover-ai-studio/, conversations are logged to sessions/, turn step traces to execution/, outcomes to outcomes/, and learned notes to growth/. Every write passes through clover_repo.redact() to scrub tokens and API keys before touching disk, and model tools are blocked in code from reading vault/ or altering clover-ai-studio/.
