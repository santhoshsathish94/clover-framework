# Correction: 'Everything Happens Inside' Questions the Fundamental Distinction Between Inside and Outside

## Summary
Clarifies that 'everything happens inside' does not mean inside an individual's private mind; rather, the discussion questions the fundamental distinction between inside and outside itself.

## Content
Corrects the earlier interpretation (MEM-HYP-INSIDE-MIND-001). Treating experience as locked 'inside an individual's mind' presupposes the very spatial/ontological divide between an inner box and an outer world that the inquiry is examining. The intended philosophical point is to question whether a fundamental boundary between 'inside' and 'outside' exists in direct experience.
