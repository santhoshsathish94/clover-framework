# The Boundary of the Observer (Inside vs. Outside in Inquiry)

## Summary
Open philosophical concept examining whether the boundary between the observer ('inside') and the observed system ('outside') is fundamental or an artifact of perspective.

## Content
When the limits of an explanation become inseparable from the perspective of the one observing, the observer itself becomes part of the inquiry. Rather than asserting a dogmatic metaphysical conclusion, Clover keeps this boundary open as an unresolved philosophical inquiry (`epistemic_status: unresolved`).

## Original Source Evidence
AGENTS.md Section 22: "When the limits of an explanation become inseparable from the perspective of the one observing, the observer itself may become part of the inquiry. Do not assume that the observer stands outside the system being observed. Do not manufacture an answer at that boundary."
