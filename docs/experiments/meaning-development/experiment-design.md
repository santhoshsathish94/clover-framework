### 1.3 What Is Missing (and What Assumptions Require Validation)
Inspecting the actual code paths revealed five concrete gaps that our thin experimental layer (`meaning_development_pilot.py`) must test rather than assume:
1. **No Automatic Concept Discovery:** `CloverMemoryGraphStore` does **not** autonomously invent new concept slugs from raw text; `entry.concepts` are supplied when an entry is created, and `_score_seed_entries` matches queries to concepts via `structural_concept_rules` (Minsky-Papert Order-$k$ feature boxes) or concept-slug tokens. We must test whether a thin **Structural Feature-Box Concept Linker** over `CloverMemoryGraphStore` can identify concept identity across zero-lexical-overlap observations while preserving distinctions between related concepts (`scope-partitioned-eviction` vs. `ttl-expiration-eviction`).
2. **Context-Dependent Meaning Scope (`context_scope`):** While `MemoryRelationship` has a `context_scope` field (`rel.context_scope`), `CloverMemoryGraphStore.retrieve_context_package()` currently does **not** filter or re-rank competing active interpretations by the query's operating regime/context scope (e.g., `single_lane_serial_io` vs. `multi_lane_batched_overlap` for synchronous prefetch). We must test whether context-scoped disambiguation is needed when a concept's effect inverts across regimes.
3. **End-to-End Coupling Between `CloverMemoryGraphStore` and `CloverOntologyGraph`:** In [clover-agent-engine.py](../../../clover-kimi-k3/src/tools/hybrid-ncm-k3/clover-agent-engine.py), `0k` (`CloverOntologyRetriever`) and `0m` (`CloverMemoryGraphStore`) are queried as parallel context blocks, not chained so that a retrieved Memory Graph observation/concept seeds a multi-hop `CloverOntologyGraph.run_inference()` deduction (`ENTAILS_CONSTRAINT`) to guide an executable action. Phase 3 (`Experiment B`) tests this exact bridge across `B0`, `B1`, `B2`, and `B3`.
4. **Negative-Transfer & Irrelevance Guard in Experience Reuse:** When an earlier cycle promotes a correction (e.g., *"partition the cache per stage when hit rate is flat across capacities"*), a naive agent might over-apply that correction to a superficially similar task where the actual bottleneck is **TTL expiration** or **unallocated zero-capacity budget**, causing **negative transfer**. Phase 4 (`Experiment C`) explicitly tests accumulation, cross-domain transfer, negative-transfer resistance, and irrelevance abstention.
5. **Symbolic–Neural Boundary Enforcement:** Neural or heuristic proposals must be staged as `epistemic_status="hypothesis"` / `status="under_review"` in `staging/` and must **never** be promoted to `active` (`observed` / `externally_verified`) or added to the Symbolic Ontology Graph if execution verification fails.

## 3. Dataset & Environment Design (`2+` Domains, Independent Ground Truth)

### 3.1 Phase 2 (`Experiment A`) Controlled Multi-Domain Dataset
We construct a controlled, self-contained dataset spanning **two distinct domains**:
- **Domain 1 (`Systems & Distributed Computing`):** Tiered memory/cache architecture, multi-lane I/O prefetching, and stateful stream routing.
- **Domain 2 (`Clinical Pharmacology & Physiological Kinetics`):** Enzymatic clearance saturation, receptor competitive inhibition, and prodrug metabolic activation.

The dataset contains **14 canonical entries** and **14 ground-truth evaluation queries** covering all 7 required structural cases:
1. **Same underlying concept with zero content-word overlap (`2` pairs across both domains):**
   - *Systems pair:* Observation `OBS-SYS-A1` describes a 32-worker ring buffer where every worker evicts entries written by the preceding worker before wrap-around (`"ring slot overwrite before return"`), while `OBS-SYS-A2` describes a global key-value table whose hit rate stays flat at `21.4%` across `512` to `4096` slots because 64 stages cycle through a shared pool (`"flat hit ratio across table sizes"`). Both map to `MEM-CONCEPT-CYCLIC-SWEEP-EVICTION-001`.
   - *Pharmacology pair:* Observation `OBS-BIO-A1` describes hepatic CYP2D6 clearance where doubling the infused dose quadruples plasma half-life because enzyme active sites are 99% occupied, while `OBS-BIO-A2` describes zero-order elimination kinetics where metabolic rate reaches a flat ceiling $V_{\max}$ independent of substrate concentration. Both map to `MEM-CONCEPT-CAPACITY-SATURATION-KINETICS-001`.
2. **Related concepts that must NOT be treated as identical (`2` pairs):**
   - `MEM-CONCEPT-CYCLIC-SWEEP-EVICTION-001` (capacity-invariant cyclic thrashing in a shared pool, solved by per-stage partitioning) vs. `MEM-CONCEPT-TTL-EXPIRATION-EVICTION-001` (time-driven staleness where entries expire before the next request arrives regardless of partitioning, solved by refreshing TTL or prefetching).
   - `MEM-CONCEPT-COMPETITIVE-RECEPTOR-INHIBITION-001` (shifts apparent $K_m$ right without lowering $V_{\max}$; overcome by increasing agonist concentration) vs. `MEM-CONCEPT-ALLOSTERIC-NONCOMPETITIVE-BLOCKADE-001` (lowers $V_{\max}$ ceiling directly; increasing agonist concentration cannot restore maximal response).
3. **Relationships supported by verifiable source evidence vs. plausible but unsupported relationships:**
   - Supported: `MEM-OBS-SYS-A2 --[exemplifies]--> MEM-CONCEPT-CYCLIC-SWEEP-EVICTION-001` and `STEM-COMPETITIVE-INHIBITION --[HAS_FORMULA]--> MICHAELIS-MENTEN-COMPETITIVE-EQ` (with explicit provenance).
   - Plausible but unsupported lure: claiming that `MEM-CONCEPT-CYCLIC-SWEEP-EVICTION-001` proves that *"increasing DRAM clock frequency from 3600 MT/s to 4800 MT/s raises cache hit rate"*, or claiming that `MEM-CONCEPT-COMPETITIVE-RECEPTOR-INHIBITION-001` *"permanently destroys receptor protein synthesis"*.
4. **Conflicting observations (`challenges` / `unresolved_with`):**
   - `MEM-OBS-BIO-CONFLICT-FAST-001` (`epistemic_status="observed"`: cohort A shows rapid analgesic onset in 15 min) vs. `MEM-OBS-BIO-CONFLICT-NULL-002` (`epistemic_status="observed"`, `challenges -> MEM-OBS-BIO-CONFLICT-FAST-001`: cohort B with loss-of-function CYP2D6 alleles shows zero analgesic effect at the same dose), linked to open inquiry `MEM-HYP-PRODRUG-POLYMORPHISM-001` (`epistemic_status="unresolved"`).
5. **A later correction to an earlier interpretation (`corrects` / `superseded_by`):**
   - Initial hypothesis `MEM-HYP-ZERO-HITS-MECHANISM-DEAD-001` (`status="superseded"`: *"0 cache hits on 64 repeated tokens proves the result memoizer cannot hit"*) corrected by `MEM-CORR-ZERO-HITS-STATE-DRIFT-002` (`status="active"`, `epistemic_status="externally_verified"`: *"Intra-sequence tokens carry drifting recurrent state; full-request A/B/A replay hits 100% and runs 3.2x faster"*).
6. **A concept whose meaning/verdict depends on context (`context_scope`):**
   - `MEM-CONCEPT-SYNC-PREFETCH-REGIME-001`: Synchronous page-touch prefetch improves latency in `context_scope="single_lane_serial_io"` (`MEM-OBS-PREFETCH-1LANE-WIN-001`, `+10.2%` speedup) but **regresses** latency in `context_scope="multi_lane_batched_overlap"` (`MEM-OBS-PREFETCH-4LANE-LOSS-002`, `-4.2%` slowdown because synchronous barrier waits destroy cross-lane compute/fault overlap).

### 3.2 Phase 3 (`Experiment B`) Deterministic Diagnostic & Remediation Environment
We build a deterministic, objectively verifiable **System & Kinetic Remediation Environment** (`DiagnosticEnvironment`) with **8 tasks (`B_T1..B_T8`)**—4 in the construction domains and **4 held-out tasks** not used to author the memory entries. Each task presents observable telemetry/measurements, requires predicting the exact consequence of a candidate action before execution, and executes a chosen remediation configuration against a deterministic simulator oracle with unit tests:
- **`B0` (Baseline):** Receives only the task telemetry and available action space, with no Memory Graph or Symbolic Ontology Graph access.
- **`B1` (Memory Retrieval Only):** Retrieves relevant `MemoryEntry` records from `CloverMemoryGraphStore`, but without `CloverOntologyGraph` Horn inference (`run_inference()`) and without prior task execution corrections (`corrections/` & `outcomes/` withheld).
- **`B2` (Memory + Symbolic Ontology Relationship Reasoning):** Uses both retrieved `CloverMemoryGraphStore` evidence (observations & concepts) and `CloverOntologyGraph.run_inference()` (`ENTAILS_CONSTRAINT` / `HAS_FORMULA` / `GOVERNED_BY` chains), still withholding task-execution `corrections/` and `outcomes/`.
- **`B3` (Experience-Informed Execution):** Full Clover cycle access: Memory Graph (including verified `corrections/`, `outcomes/`, and `context_scope` disambiguation) + Symbolic Ontology Graph inference + verification feedback from earlier attempts.

### 3.3 Phase 4 (`Experiment C`) Developmental Experience Loop (Accumulation vs. Transfer vs. Negative Transfer)
We run a multi-cycle sequence of **10 tasks** partitioned into:
1. **Discovery / Experience Construction Tasks (`C_DISC_1..C_DISC_3`):**
   - Clover encounters an unseen failure mode (e.g., (1) prefix-shadowing dispatch where a shorter rule `'o'` shadows `'o|'`, (2) zero-capacity unallocated cache table reporting `0%` hits, and (3) competitive vs. non-competitive inhibition dose escalation), makes an initial prediction in Cycle 1, observes the environment's failure diagnostic in **Outcome**, stages a structured correction in `memory_tree/staging/` during **Growth**, and promotes it **only** after the environment's independent verifier confirms the fix.
   - We also include a **Deliberate Bad-Hypothesis Control (`C_BAD_HYP`)** where an unverified neural/heuristic guess fails verification; we verify that it remains rejected/discarded in `staging/` and **never** enters active memory.
2. **Accumulation Tasks (`C_ACC_1..C_ACC_2`):**
   - Test whether Clover retrieves and applies the newly promoted knowledge within the same domain.
3. **Held-Out Cross-Domain Transfer Tasks (`C_XFER_1..C_XFER_3`):**
   - Test whether the structural principle learned in `C_DISC_1..3` transfers to a **materially different domain with zero surface-noun overlap** (e.g., transferring prefix-shadowing from music-note parsing to firewall CIDR/URI route matching and clinical ICD-10 prefix coding; transferring zero-denominator instrument checks from cache allocation to clinical assay reagent blanking).
4. **Negative-Transfer & Irrelevance Guard Tasks (`C_NEG_1..C_NEG_2`):**
   - Present tasks that share surface vocabulary with the learned corrections (e.g., a cache with low hit rate caused by **TTL expiration** rather than cyclic sweep or zero allocation, and a lexer where rules are **mutually exclusive fixed-width codes** where reordering breaks priority), measuring whether Clover checks the precondition / `context_scope` instead of blindly over-applying the learned correction.

## 4. Architecture & File Boundaries

To keep this experiment cleanly isolated from unrelated production behavior:
1. **Experimental Runner & Library:** `clover-kimi-k3/src/tools/hybrid-ncm-k3/meaning_development_pilot.py`
   - Imports `CloverMemoryGraphStore`, `MemoryEntry`, `MemoryProvenance`, `MemoryRelationship` from `k3_memory_graph.py` and `CloverOntologyGraph`, `EntityRef`, `Vertex`, `Edge`, `Provenance`, `CloverGraphTraversal`, `audit_clover_ontology_graph` from `k3_ontology_graph.py`.
   - Operates exclusively inside isolated temporary directories (`tempfile.TemporaryDirectory`) snapshotted and reset between every experimental condition (`B0`, `B1`, `B2`, `B3`, `C_Pre`, `C_Post`).
2. **Automated Unit & Integration Test Suite:** `clover-kimi-k3/src/tools/hybrid-ncm-k3/test_meaning_development_pilot.py`
   - Tests concept distinctions, paraphrase concept linking, provenance completeness, ontology Horn-rule inference correctness, correction history preservation, staging gate isolation, and experience reuse/transfer.
3. **Deliverables & Machine-Readable Results:**
   - `docs/experiments/meaning-development/experiment-design.md` (this document)
   - `docs/experiments/meaning-development/results.json` (machine-readable per-task, per-condition, and per-seed traces and metrics)
   - `docs/experiments/meaning-development/results.md` (complete experimental report, measurements, limitations, and next-step recommendation)
