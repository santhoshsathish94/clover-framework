## 2. What the Book Claims & Its Enactive Formalism

### 2.1 From Disembodied Cognitivism to Enaction
Varela, Thompson, and Rosch critique classical cognitivism (which treats cognition as disembodied symbol manipulation recovering pre-given properties of an observer-independent world) and formulate the **Enactive Approach** around two core principles (`Chapters 8–9`):
1. **Perception as Perceptually Guided Action (Sensorimotor Coupling)**:
   - Sensory inputs $s_t$ and motor actions $a_t$ form a closed dynamical loop ($\Delta s_t = J(w_t) a_t$). How the perceiver moves determines the invariants of what is perceived (exemplified by Held & Hein's 1963 active vs. passive kitten experiment).
2. **Autopoietic Sense-Making ("Bringing Forth a World")**:
   - Cognitive structures and meaningful categories (`food`, `threat`, `obstacle`) are not pre-labeled in physics; they emerge from the recurrent sensorimotor patterns that enable an operationally closed organism to maintain its viability.

## 3. Executable Experiments Run & Observed Results

We implemented both enactive experiments in [run_varela_thompson_rosch_1991_embodied_mind_experiments.py](../../../research/consciousness-and-subjective-experience/17-varela-thompson-rosch-1991-embodied-mind/run_varela_thompson_rosch_1991_embodied_mind_experiments.py):

### Experiment E1: Held & Hein (1963) Active Enactive Agent vs. Yoked Passive Observer on the Visual Cliff
- **Setup**: Two agents receive the **exact same visual optic-flow stream** $\dot{s}_t = \frac{1}{d_t} a_t + \epsilon_t$ over 400 training steps and 200 held-out Visual Cliff test trials (`shallow safe depth = 1.0` vs. `deep cliff depth = 6.0`):
  - The **Enactive Self-Moving Agent** selects its own motor velocity $a_t$ and correlates $(\dot{s}_t, a_t)$.
  - The **Yoked Passive Observer** receives the identical $\dot{s}_t$ visual sequence frame-for-frame (carried in a gondola without access to self-generated motor commands $a_t$).
- **Observed Results**:
  - **Enactive Self-Moving Agent Visual Cliff Accuracy**: **`100.0%` (`1.0000`)**.
  - **Yoked Passive Observer Visual Cliff Accuracy**: **`58.5%` (`0.5850`, near chance)** — proving mathematically that passive exposure to identical sensory data without sensorimotor agency fails to disambiguate environmental depth from ego-motion.

### Experiment E2: Autopoietic Sense-Making — Opposite Enacted Significance Worlds in the Same Physical Environment
- **Setup**: Placed two organisms (`Organism_A`: glycolytic, regenerated by Compound X and degraded by Compound Y; `Organism_B`: lithotrophic, regenerated by Compound Y and degraded by Compound X) in the **exact same 40-zone chemical environment** $(C_x, C_y)$.
- **Observed Results**:
  - `Organism_A` enacts significance vector **`[+0.8695, -0.8257]`**;
  - `Organism_B` enacts significance vector **`[-0.8019, +0.8346]`**;
  - Cosine similarity between the two organisms' enacted affordance worlds in the identical physical space is **`-0.9930`**.

## 4. Surprises, Limitations & Epistemic Boundary

1. **Why Passive Training on Recorded Trajectories Cannot Replace Closed-Loop Sensorimotor Agency**: Experiment E1 shows with a 1-line equation ($\dot{s}_t = d_t^{-1} a_t$) why passive observation of sensory streams is underdetermined whenever the sensory observation is a bilinear product of an unknown environmental property ($d_t^{-1}$) and an unobserved motor intervention ($a_t$).
