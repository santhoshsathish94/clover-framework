# Consolidated 17-Paper Research Guide: Subjective Experience, Phenomenal Consciousness & Artificial Consciousness

Offered, not imposed. Date: 2026-11-09. Direction owner: Santhosh.

> **Core Epistemic Distinction**: None of these 17 papers establishes that any current or near-term AI system is conscious. Together, they provide (1) philosophical analyses of why subjective experience (*what it is like*) resists purely functional reduction, (2) neuroscientific theories and adversarial predictions about how consciousness arises in biological brains, (3) no-report and covert physiological paradigms for measuring consciousness without confounding it with overt report, (4) provisional computational indicator rubrics and mimicry/internal-variant safeguards for evaluating AI architectures, and (5) interoceptive, homeostatic, and enactive accounts of embodied feeling.
