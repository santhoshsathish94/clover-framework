# Research

Research informs Clover; it is not automatically framework doctrine or a runnable product.
Historical records retain the measurements, corrections, and failed attempts that produced
later conclusions. A document's status describes its role, not whether its outcome was favorable.

| Topic | Start here | Role and limit |
|---|---|---|
| AI-Fire | [Research summary](ai-fire/README.md) | Dependency and concentration argument; source-ledger work remains open |
| AI future | [The hypothesis](ai-future/README.md) | Speculation with explicit limits; independent of adopting the framework |
| Clover AI | [Implementation direction](clover-ai/README.md) | Proposed bounded engineering system, not a completed runtime |
| Kimi K3 | [Investigation index](k3/README.md) | Model derivation, hardware measurements, experiments, and handoff records |

For maintained framework guidance, use [docs](../docs/README.md). For the executable
single-machine reference, use [Clover-K3](../clover-k3/README.md). For reported real work,
use [case studies](../case-studies/README.md).

New research should state its question, evidence environment and date, what was observed,
what remains unknown, and which current summary it informs. Preserve historical evidence;
do not update an old run's claims to make it look like a later run.
