## 3. Specific Code & Mathematical Insights Extracted in Stage 1

### 3.1 Why Weight Federation (`FedAvg`) Is the Wrong Starting Point for Clover
1. **Model heterogeneity in Clover (`Paper 3`):** Clover runs `LFM3-Omni-2B` (24 tri-hybrid layers) on mobile/laptop, `Nemotron 3.5 Super VL` (52 Mamba-2 + LatentMoE layers), and `Kimi K3` (93 KDA + MLA + 896-expert layers) on servers. Parameter averaging across these architectures is undefined.
2. **Sparse lexical gradient leakage (`Paper 1`, Footnote 1):** On sparse text/intent features, non-zero gradients directly reveal the exact words typed by the user.
3. **Unlearning ripple effects (`Paper 10`):** When personal data is baked into continuous weights, removing one user's data cannot be done without expensive retraining, and retraining can expose other users' data.

### 3.2 What Voyager (`Paper 4`) and Reflexion (`Paper 5`) Get Right—and Where Their Code Breaks in a Multi-User Collective Setting
From reading `voyager/agents/skill.py` and `reflexion/programming_runs/reflexion.py`:
- **What they get right:** Both represent learned capability as **explicit, inspectable, non-parametric artifacts** (Voyager: executable skills indexed by semantic description; Reflexion: linguistic reflections induced from failure feedback) that improve an agent without retraining model weights.
- **Where they break if used directly for collective learning:**
  1. **Unvalidated overwrite (`Voyager` `skill.py:65-72`):** A skill that succeeds once in one user's local environment immediately enters `skills.json` and overwrites any prior skill of the same name. In a population of users, a user's personal habit (e.g., *"tidy a file means delete it"*) or a poisoned rule would overwrite general behavior.
  2. **Instance-bound reflections (`Reflexion` `generate_reflections.py:12-28`):** Reflexion prompts include the literal episode transcript (`scenario`). Exporting raw reflections leaks private text and fails to generalize when object names change.
  3. **Self-test blind spot (`Reflexion` `reflexion.py:36-43`):** When an agent evaluates its own candidate only on its own context (`tests_i`), it exits early on false positives. MAML (`maml.py:66-101`) shows why the outer loop must evaluate on a **disjoint query set (`inputb`)** drawn from independent tasks.

### 3.3 A Novel Discovery from Connecting Paper 10 (`Privacy Ripple Effects`) to Clover's `personal.py`
In Clover's existing [`personal.py`](../../clover-ai-system/mobile/personal.py) (`Learner._generalise`, lines 265–281), a personal hypothesis generalizes by taking the **set intersection** of features across supporting experiences:
$$T_{\text{merged}} = T_1 \cap T_2$$
Suppose Experience 1 on a user's device is `"stash the secret_merger_acmecorp notes"` with features $\{\text{tok=stash}, \text{tok=secret\_merger\_acmecorp}, \text{tok=note}\}$, and Experience 2 is `"stash the budget memo"` with features $\{\text{tok=stash}, \text{tok=budget}, \text{tok=memo}\}$.
- While both experiences exist, their intersection is $T_{\text{merged}} = \{\text{tok=stash}\}$—the private token `secret_merger_acmecorp` is scrubbed by intersection!
- **Now suppose Experience 2 is deleted or excluded (`stop_learning` / consent withdrawal), or suppose intersection happens across two contributors and Contributor 2 withdraws:**
  When `Learner.rebuild` replays Experience 1 alone, the hypothesis trigger reverts to $\{\text{tok=stash}, \text{tok=secret\_merger\_acmecorp}, \text{tok=note}\}$—**unmasking the private token `secret_merger_acmecorp`!**
- Furthermore, in collective aggregation, if a valid general strategy $S_{\text{general}}$ (supported by $K=3$ users) outranks a narrower, overfitted or borderline candidate $S_{\text{narrow}}$, and one contributor to $S_{\text{general}}$ withdraws consent (dropping $S_{\text{general}}$ to $K=2 < K_{\min}$), $S_{\text{narrow}}$ can become active (**a symbolic unlearning ripple effect**).
- **Consequence for Clover's Collective Architecture:**
  1. Local candidate extraction (`collective.py`) must enforce a **closed general-vocabulary & CSI grammar/dictionary filter before export** (never relying on multi-example intersection alone to hide private tokens).
  2. Collective promotion (`collective_evaluator.py`) must validate every candidate independently against a held-out regression & generalisation suite, so withdrawing one candidate never activates an unvalidated fallback.

## 4. Next Movement (Stage 2)

To understand these mechanisms by **running and observing them** rather than reading alone, Stage 2 builds and executes controlled, reproducible experiments on `k3` testing each of the five core paper mechanisms and their failure modes:
- **M1 (`FedAvg` vs. `Federated Analytics` on Non-IID & Sparse Lexical Features):** Reproducing McMahan et al. (2017) Footnote 1 gradient-support leakage vs. Wang et al. (2022) Federated Analytics with privacy filtering.
- **M2 (`Voyager` Skill Library & Last-Writer-Wins vs. Held-Out Verification):** Running Voyager's skill-indexing and retrieval pattern (`voyager/agents/skill.py`) on multi-step problem solving, demonstrating both its compositional speedup and its vulnerability to unvalidated skill overwrite.
- **M3 (`Reflexion` Verbal Reinforcement & Self-Test Blind Spot vs. Abstracted Strategy Transfer):** Running Reflexion's `Actor -> Evaluator -> Self-Reflection` loop (`reflexion.py`), comparing raw instance-bound reflections against CSI-abstracted generalizable strategies across unseen problems.
- **M4 (`MAML` Support/Query Split + `Robust FL` Poisoning/Sybil Defense):** Comparing naive frequency aggregation against Swarm/Robust-FL installation-deduplicated quorum + MAML-style held-out query evaluation under Sybil (1 user $\times 10$ reports), personal-idiosyncratic habits, and Byzantine poisoned contributions.
- **M5 (`Privacy Ripple Effects` in Continual & Symbolic Unlearning):** Reproducing Borkar et al. (2025)'s `taxonomy.py` assisted memorization & removal ripple effects, and testing our newly discovered **intersection-unmasking & shadow-promotion ripple effects** with and without Clover's closed-vocabulary + independent-validation safeguards.
