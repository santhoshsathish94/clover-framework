Story rating

Each item is a short children's story written for a story plan. Rate it on two separate 1-5 scales. Rate each item on its own; do not try to guess where it came from. You can stop at any time (progress is saved in this browser; use Download at the end or whenever you stop).

Fluency (is it clean English?): 1 = many garbled or made-up words, doubled words, or repeated phrases; 3 = readable with several glitches; 5 = reads like a clean story, no glitches.
Plan (does it follow the plan?): 1 = unrelated to the plan; 3 = some plan elements, others missing or wrong order; 5 = covers the plan's events in order.
