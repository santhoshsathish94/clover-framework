# Dialogue on Perception and the Meaning of 'Everything Happens Inside'

## Summary
Recorded philosophical dialogue examining the statement 'everything happens inside', first interpreted as mental enclosure and subsequently clarified as questioning the boundary between 'inside' and 'outside' itself.

## Content
In Turn 1, the human remarked: 'When you look closely at experience, everything happens inside.' In Turn 2, an initial summary interpreted this as the claim that all perceived events occur inside an individual's private mind. In Turn 5, the human corrected that reading, clarifying that calling experience 'inside' only makes sense relative to an assumed 'outside', and that the inquiry actually questions the fundamental distinction between inside and outside.

## Original Source Evidence
Turn 1 (Human): "When we observe any sound, colour, or thought, everything happens inside."
Turn 2 (Assistant - initial reading): "So you mean everything we experience happens inside an individual's mind."
Turn 5 (Human - clarification): "No — saying 'inside an individual's mind' still assumes a neat box with an outside. The point of saying 'everything happens inside' is to question the fundamental distinction between inside and outside altogether."
